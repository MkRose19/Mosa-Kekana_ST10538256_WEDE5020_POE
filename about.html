About Us

Our History

Founded to honor the legacy of Tebogo Theron, our foundation started in 2020 to address poverty in Greater Tubatse communities.

Mission

To empower vulnerable families through sustainable programs in education, health, food security and skills development.

Vision

A thriving Greater Tubatse where every child has opportunity and every family has dignity.
